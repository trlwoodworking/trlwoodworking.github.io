TRL Office Privacy Policy

TRL Woodworking, LLC. Last updated November 24, 2026.

TRL Office is a private program that runs on the owner's own computer. Its only user is the owner of TRL Woodworking, LLC, who connects it to the business Google account. This page explains what it does with that Google account.

What it uses from Google

Gmail, sending. TRL Office sends emails the owner writes or approves, such as invoices, proposals and material lists, with their PDF files attached. They go out from the owner's Gmail and show in its Sent folder.

Gmail, headers only. TRL Office looks at the sender, subject and date of recent messages in the inbox. If a client, subcontractor or supplier is waiting on a reply, it puts a reminder on the owner's calendar. It never reads what an email says, and it never changes, moves or deletes email.

Google Calendar. TRL Office creates one calendar called TRL Jobs and keeps job dates and reminders on it. It can't see any other calendar.

Google Sheets. TRL Office reads and adds rows to one spreadsheet, the business's own document number register.

Where the information stays

Everything TRL Office gets from Google is kept on the owner's computer and in the owner's own Google account. It is not sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Nobody other than the owner has access to it.

TRL Office's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Turning it off

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's Third-party connections page, or with the Disconnect button in TRL Office.
